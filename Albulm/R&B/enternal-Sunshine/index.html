Ariana Grande - Eternal Sunshine

Eternal Sunshine is the seventh studio album by American singer Ariana Grande. It was released in 2024 by Republic Records. The album showcases Grande’s versatility across pop and R&B, blending upbeat anthems with introspective ballads. Its artwork and aesthetic have been described as ethereal and luminous, drawing inspiration from sun-drenched visuals, retro-futuristic themes, and vibrant color palettes reminiscent of summer skies and glowing cityscapes.
